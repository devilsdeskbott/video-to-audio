Video to audio

Drop videos you’ve saved from any platform and get the sound as MP3 or WAV. MP4 videos of any length, even many hours, are processed in small pieces, so memory never runs out. Nothing is uploaded.

Output settings

Keep this tab open while a long video converts. Normalize and cut silence read the file twice, so they take longer. MP4, M4A and MOV files with AAC audio (what most platforms give you) have no length limit. Other formats such as WebM or MKV are loaded fully into memory, so very long ones may fail: convert those to MP4 first. Pasting a link doesn’t work here, because browsers block pages from fetching other sites’ videos. Save the video first. Only convert videos you own or have permission to use.
